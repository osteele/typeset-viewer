# Adaptive quadrature screenshot fixture

`main.typ` is a synthetic paper for Typeset Viewer screenshots. Its structure
populates the thumbnail, outline, figures, references, and notes sidebars. The
results are illustrative and make no research claim.

The sentence beginning “The local error indicator” is the stable selection and
note anchor. To check relocation, attach a note to that sentence, insert a new
paragraph above it, and save. The note should follow the quoted text after the
next render. Deleting the sentence should retain the note with a “Not found in
current render” status.

Keep filenames, headings, captions, citations, and the anchor sentence stable.
Changing them invalidates more screenshots than a normal prose edit.
