\section{Error estimates}

Adaptive quadrature concentrates evaluations where the estimated error is
largest. The source remains an ordinary \LaTeX{} project while the rendered
paper stays open beside the editor.

\subsection{Stopping rule}

For intervals $I_k$, stop when
\[
  \sum_k \lvert E_k \rvert < \varepsilon.
\]
